FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything we get asked by Sydney wholesalers, distributors and importers before they switch.

How much does Inventory Pro cost?

Inventory Pro is $50/month flat. No per-user fees, no setup fees, no minimum contract. Unlimited users, unlimited warehouses, unlimited SKUs. You can cancel anytime.

Can you import our existing stock data from Excel?

Yes. We help you import your existing product list, suppliers, customers and on-hand quantities during your onboarding session. Most teams come in from spreadsheets — it's a standard part of getting started.

Can we manage multiple warehouses?

Absolutely. Inventory Pro supports multiple warehouses from day one. Inter-warehouse transfers are fully tracked, you can see stock-on-hand per location, and reports roll up to the business level whenever you need them.

Does Inventory Pro support barcode scanners?

Barcode scanning is on the roadmap — we're building native support for standard USB and Bluetooth scanners for receiving, stocktakes, picking and transfers. Today, most customers run stocktakes using SKU lookup on tablets or count sheets, then batch-adjust in Inventory Pro. We'll import scanned data for you during onboarding if you already have a system.

Can we use it on phones and tablets in the warehouse?

Yes. Inventory Pro is fully responsive, so it works smoothly on any modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop or desktop. Many customers use cheap Android tablets in the warehouse for receiving and stocktakes.

Is our data secure?

Yes. All data is transferred over HTTPS and stored securely. Every data-changing action writes to a full audit trail so you can see who changed what, and when. This system is built to run sensitive commercial inventory data.

How long until we're up and running?

Most customers are live within a few working days. Your first 15-minute demo → we send you a simple data import template → we help you import → you start using the system. No 6-week implementations.

Who is this NOT for?

Inventory Pro is built for Australian SMBs managing physical stock. It's not for pure retail POS-only use cases, and it's not for large enterprises wanting custom 3-month implementations. If you have any doubt, just book a free demo and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.
